Why quality survives

The download is the original resolution with an alpha channel added - RGB bytes are copied from the source untouched, never re-encoded from a preview. The AI model runs via WebAssembly on your machine (U‑2‑Net, Apache‑2.0); no image data ever leaves the page. For webcams use OBS's chroma key - this is for images.
